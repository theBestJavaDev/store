Questions

Will these parts fit my goBILDA or REV robot?

Yes. Bearings, collars, and shafts follow the standard 8 mm and 6 mm sizes used in FTC, and plate templates use the 8 mm hole pattern. Each listing shows exact dimensions.

Are your parts competition legal?

They are commercial off-the-shelf mechanical parts and custom-cut plates, which FTC rules allow. Always check the current season’s Competition Manual before your event.

Which plate material should I pick?

Aluminum for drivetrain plates and anything that takes load. SRPP for flexible, impact-resistant guards and intakes. Wood for prototypes and quick fit checks.

How do school purchase orders work?

Check “Pay by school PO” in the cart, then send your signed PO to business@seattlesolvers.com. We ship on approval and invoice Net-30.

Can I pick up locally?

Yes, choose local pickup in the cart and we’ll email a time to meet in Sammamish.
